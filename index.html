Our history

A college built on purpose.

Malik Bin Anas College of West Africa was established to provide accessible, quality education rooted in discipline, knowledge, and values. From its earliest beginnings, the institution set out to support students in achieving academic success while developing leadership, responsibility, and social awareness.

Over the years, the college has grown into a respected learning center, creating opportunities for students to pursue meaningful academic and personal growth. It continues to empower learners, strengthen local communities, and prepare the next generation for a brighter future.
